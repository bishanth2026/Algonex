AI that answers from your own data

Turn the documents you already own into answers

Most of what a business needs to know is already written down — in contracts, tickets, wikis and reports nobody has time to read. We build a retrieval layer on top of the data you already hold, so your team gets answers with the source attached instead of guessing.

  • Answers your team can act on, with the source document and passage attached to every claim
  • Connected to the systems that already hold the material — wikis, ticket history, contracts, databases — and re-indexed as they change
  • Your access rules carried through, so nobody can retrieve a document they could not already open
  • A graded question set from the first week, so accuracy is measured when a model or a prompt changes, not assumed

If the answer is not in your material, the honest response is to say so. We build that in deliberately — a system that guesses once is a system nobody trusts twice.

We connect to the systems that already hold your material, index it with permissions intact, and prove accuracy against a question set your own people grade — before anything goes near production. Talk through your data

Where teams put it to work

  • Support and service desk

    Agents answer from product documentation, policy and past tickets in one place, and send a reply with the source attached instead of hunting across five tabs.

  • Internal knowledge and onboarding

    New joiners ask the company's own runbooks and wikis rather than interrupting the one person who remembers. The knowledge stops living in a handful of heads.

  • Bids and proposals

    Past proposals, security questionnaires and pricing sheets become searchable, so a bid team reuses what has already been answered instead of rewriting the paragraph from scratch.

  • Contracts, policy and compliance

    What a clause, a regulation or an internal policy actually says, with the passage cited so a reviewer can check it — the case where a confidently wrong answer costs the most.

AI that acts in your systems

From answering questions to doing the work

Retrieval answers questions from your documents. MCP acts in your systems. Most teams end up wanting both.

Retrieval tells your team what the contract says. MCP lets an assistant act on it — look up the account, raise the order, update the record — in the system of record itself, and as the person asking. We design the server, scope its permissions, and roll it out to the clients your team already uses.

It acts, it doesn't just answer
Your systems are exposed as explicit, typed operations, so the assistant calls a real API instead of guessing at a screen. Reads and writes both — with a person approving anything that changes data.
Build once, works everywhere
MCP is an open protocol. One server serves Claude, the IDE assistants and your own agents, so supporting a new client is a configuration change rather than another integration project.
Safe by construction
Every call carries the caller's identity, so an assistant can only reach what the person using it could already reach — and you get a log of everything it did.

How we work

From first conversation to live system — four phases, no surprises

Each phase ends with a decision point. If the prototype does not earn the next phase, we say so — a cheap outcome, and a far better one than a year of sunk cost.

  1. 01

    Discover

    1–2 weeks

    We map the workflow, the systems that hold the data, and the constraints that are actually non-negotiable. You get a written scope and a fixed estimate before anyone writes code.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    2–4 weeks

    A narrow slice, running against your real data. Enough to prove the approach works — and cheap enough to throw away if it does not.

  3. 03

    Harden

    4–10 weeks

    Tests, evaluation sets, access control, error handling and CI. This is the phase most AI projects skip, and it is the reason most of them never reach production.

  4. 04

    Operate

    Ongoing

    Monitoring, cost tracking and iteration. We hand over documentation and infrastructure you own outright — including the option to run it without us.

Engineering principles

Built right from the start — every time, on every project

Types, evaluation, telemetry and access control are cheap to design in and painful to add later. We build them in on day one — not as optional extras, not after the first incident.

Typed end to end

One schema from the database to the API to the browser. If a contract changes, the build fails before your users do.

Evaluated, not guessed

Every retrieval system ships with a graded question set. We can tell you what accuracy changed when a prompt or a model changed.

Observable by default

Structured logs, traces and cost telemetry go in on day one — not after the first production incident forces the issue.

Least privilege

Tools and retrievers inherit the caller's permissions. An assistant can never read a document the person asking could not open themselves.
